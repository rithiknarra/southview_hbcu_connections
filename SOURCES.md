## Connection eligibility audit — October 20, 2026

Donation-only and honorary-degree-only connections are excluded. Attendance alone or academy/high-school completion does not establish college graduation. Naming does not imply a financial contribution. Existing source inventory above preserves historical research even for removed entries.
